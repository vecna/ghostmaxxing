Vision & About

Make the machine-readable face contestable.

Ghostmaxxing is a public research lab for testing face-recognition camouflage in the browser, documenting what actually works, and investigating the biometric systems already installed in public space.

It is not an invisibility product and it does not sell protection. It is a test bench, an archive of what has already been tried, and a way for people who know how these systems are built, bought or operated to reach us.

What's Ghostmaxxing.

One research loop, not four features sharing a website. Each stage produces what the next one needs.

Play with makeup (or 3D stuff!)...

In the browser lab, with water-based makeup, printed patterns or anything else worth trying. A result can be shared, and also packaged as a Ghostyle, a modular AR intervention others can apply through the lab to retest. Every result is local: you share it only if you want to.


...the dream: make it fashionable!

Allies might report what is really deployed.

This lab runs on open-source components, and it alone cannot tell us what is deployed out there. If you build, sell, operate, approve or encounter face recognition, you know which technologies are in circulation, and whether our camouflage is being tested against anything close to them. Leak to us safely. We need it.

The Fediverse to amplify and align.

Let's find the least invasive method to break face recognition. A federated social network is the natural environment for sharing results, inviting replications and learning more. Everything this project touches gets reported there. Follow it there.

This is the complexity of Ghostmaxxing: face-recognition abuses come from a political inability to challenge the techno-securitarian state. It is not just software. It wants to be a method: to raise awareness, talk to new audiences, and work globally.

Why Ghostmaxxing?

Face recognition arrived in public space pushed by the idea that it is an advantage, for collective security and for personal benefit. That is already debatable, and the evidence of abuse keeps piling up, but meanwhile it is getting normalised through consumer products.

A ban that keeps getting reopened.

In Europe, real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law enforcement is treated as a prohibited AI practice, subject to narrow exceptions. The Reclaim Your Face campaign spent years getting that prohibition written, but none of this matters to the surveillance state. It starts with "special" cases, then pilot deployments, then the Data Protection Authority blocks them, and then the surveillance state cripples the Data Protection Authority. It would not be a fight if it were that easy.

What we need: informants!

The reporting node runs on GlobaLeaks, and there is a dedicated questionnaire for each kind of informant.

Read the safety guidance on the reporting page before you write anything. Describe what you saw in your own words rather than uploading originals where you can, and do not continue if doing so would put you at risk.

Who is behind it.

Ghostmaxxing was developed by Claudio Agosti for the 2026 NINA festival, where it ran as Ghòstati (become a ghost), a workshop held with the participation of Michelle Tylicki. Tylicki had been running adversarial makeup workshops before us: her installation DAZZLE, developed with Lauri Love, is an art-and-tech piece for teaching Computer Vision Dazzle. We added the application, and learned the workshop from people who had already been doing it.

It started as a tool to support experimental workshops, but then I reframed it as Ghostmaxxing in an attempt to make the personal experiment part of a collective one. I added the submission platform, the Fediverse integration, the references selection, the localisation support, and the ambition of making it a worldwide archive of effective camouflage. That is why it became a standalone project, though there is still no clear roadmap.
