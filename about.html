Vision & About

Contest the spreading of biometric surveillance.

Ghostmaxxing is a public research lab for testing face-recognition camouflage in the browser, documenting what actually works, and investigating the biometric systems already installed in public space.

Use the lab to test camouflage, the archive to find prior work, and the reporting channel to share evidence of deployments and abuse.

Why Ghostmaxxing?

Face recognition is sold as security and convenience, despite evidence of abuse. Consumer products are making it commonplace.

The challenge of Ghostmaxxing is socio-politica! Face-recognition abuses come the collective inability to challenge the techno-securitarian story we keep getting sold. It is not just software what's deploy in cameras, and so is not just software what we need to change it. Ghostmaxxing wants to be a method: to raise awareness, talk to new audiences, and work globally.

Who is behind it.

Ghostmaxxing is free software, and all contributions are welcome. Your friendly techno-activist Vecna is maintaining it as part of an attempt to explore how a concept born locally can be global. The project began in spring 2026 for the NINA festival, where it was known as Ghòstati ("become a ghost"), which was part of an even more visionary experimental concept called the Universal Digital Union. The first workshop was held with Michelle Tylicki's participation. Michelle had previous experience in adversarial makeup and had hosted the DAZZLE installation.

It has since been used by NINA and others to assist with adversarial makeup workshops. Ghostmaxxing is an effort to make this concept global. After all, we need more facial diversity and more adopters in a variety of contexts to gain a meaningful understanding. For this attempt, I have redesigned the software to version 1.x, adding the whistleblowing submission platform, the Fediverse integration, the research references, and a growing list of complementary projects. The roadmap for future improvements is only briefly defined. Expect tools to help analyse (other technologies or aspects) of face recognition as part of the mandate.

Ghostmaxxing is now a standalone project. NINA organises workshops and handles whistleblowing reports.

Vision / Goals / Wishes

  • Make adversarial makeup a popular practice.
  • Make workshops easier to run.
  • Test existing clothing, 3D-printed objects, hats and lights.
  • Compare camouflage results and how much they change appearance.
  • Document weaknesses in deployed face-recognition systems.

How Does Ghostmaxxing Work?

Test camouflage

Try makeup, privacy clothing or another intervention. Share results so others can retest them.
Every result is local: you share it only if you want to.


Open the lab

Report deployments

This lab uses open-source components, and it cannot establish performance against deployed systems. People who build, sell, operate or encounter them can provide evidence through the reporting channel.

Read the reporting guidance

Share and retest

It's a collective effort to find the least invasive method to break face recognition. A federated content sharing network is the natural environment for pushing and pulling results, inviting replications and learning more.


Follow it there

Challenge surveillance policy

In Europe, real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law enforcement is treated as a prohibited AI practice, subject to narrow exceptions. The Reclaim Your Face campaign fought for that prohibition, but the surveillance state won. Ghostmaxxing investigates deployments and challenges their normalisation.

Informants wanted! How to safely report?

The reporting node runs on GlobaLeaks, and there is a dedicated questionnaire for each kind of informant.

Read the safety guidance on the reporting page before you write anything. Describe what you saw in your own words rather than uploading originals where you can, and do not continue if doing so would put you at risk.
