Workshops

Bring your face and curiosity.

Hands-on sessions where we test local face-recognition behaviour together. Exchange tips, get quick suggestions, all while trying to avoid covering your face with so much paint that even your friends would not recognize you. The goal is to fool machines, not people.

All you need is face painting suitable for kids, and water based, for example:

Start painting, try to break the lines of the face. At workshop usually pairs of people paint each other.

This is how a success looks like, when the square isn't around your face, but just confused into a corner

You can also use mobile app that apply filter, if they say "Find a face", well, you did it!

The issue is that is too easy to make a clownshow, the aim is to use least paint as possible

In this page we should connect the availability of artist/activist and the interest of groups to get a workshop.

If you want to host a workshop in your space, or, you've a community of interested individuals, just comeback here but at the moment this page isn't ready to collect the interest from all around the world, still, if any facilitator wants to offer their availability,

What's missing?

  1. A map/list of future workshops.
  2. A method for artists/activists to introduce themselves, be vetted, so we can connect them

And if you want to do it on your own?

  1. Buy water based colors suitable for kids
  2. Read the References to understand the state of the art
  3. Try it out, you'll see it's pretty simple
  4. If you have a success you want to share, please consider recording a 1-second video and/or pictures, so we can share it, talk about it, and try to replicate it
