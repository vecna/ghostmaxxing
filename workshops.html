Workshops

Bring a laptop and curiosity.

Hands-on sessions where we test local face-recognition behaviour together — enrol a baseline face, change it, and watch the match move. Everything runs in the browser on your own machine.

Upcoming sessions

Nothing scheduled publicly at the moment. If you want to host a session, or want to know when the next one lands, get in touch.

What a session covers

A working outline, not a promise. The lab is the material; the argument is what a local result does and does not tell you about a deployed system.

  • Enrol a baseline face and read what the pipeline actually measures.
  • Apply a Ghostyle, or paint one, and retest under the room's real lighting.
  • Read the failure honestly — a local miss is not protection anywhere else.

What you need

A laptop with a webcam and a recent browser. No account, no install, no upload. If you would rather not use your own face, the lab takes a still image instead.

Open the lab if you want to try the material first.
