Ghostmaxxing is a public browser lab for testing face-recognition camouflage. Point your camera, change how your face looks, and watch an open-source recognition pipeline react. Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to share a result.

Built for hands-on workshops and self-experiments.

Open the lab ↗

How it works

1. Save

Save your face while it's clean — a private baseline, kept on your device, to test camouflage against.

2. Paint

Apply a Ghostyle or your own intervention — makeup, an overlay, a physical change to the face.

3. Upload

Share your result to the gallery or generate a report — entirely optional, and always your call.
