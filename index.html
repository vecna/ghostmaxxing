What the lab is for

Disrupt face recognition, one Ghostyle at a time

    Open the lab ↗

    Ghostmaxxing is your personal laboratory for testing any kind of camouflage. Use your phone or webcam, change how your face looks (makeup, clothing, prosthetics…), and measure how face recognition reacts. Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it, unless you choose to share a success in the public gallery.

    Built for hands-on workshops and self-experiments.

    How it works

    1. Save the baseline (clean face)

    Save your face while it's clean. A private baseline, kept on your device, to test camouflage against.

    2. Paint until camouflaged

    Apply a Ghostyle or your own intervention: makeup, an overlay, a physical change to the face.

    3. (Optional) Upload

    Share your result to the gallery. Together we will find the most subtle, least invasive, and still effective camouflage.
