What the lab is for

One Ghostyle, three faces.

    Distances are measured in the browser lab against its own face-recognition model, on frontal frames in even light. A distance past the threshold means that model stopped matching that frame. It is not protection, and it does not transfer to other systems. The faces are synthetic and carry the AI Gen label.

    Disrupt Face Recognition

    Ghostmaxxing is your personal laboratory for testing any type of camouflage. Uses mobile or webcam, change how your face looks (with makeup, clotching, phrostetics...), and measure how the face recognition reacts. Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves it
    ...     unless you choose to share your success! (public archive)

    Built for hands-on workshops and self-experiments.

    How it works

    1. Save the baseline (clean face)

    Save your face while it's clean. A private baseline, kept on your device, to test camouflage against.

    2. Paint until camouflaged

    Apply a Ghostyle or your own intervention: makeup, an overlay, a physical change to the face.

    3. (Optional) Upload

    Share your result to the gallery, we want to see the least invasive, and yet effective, camouflage!
