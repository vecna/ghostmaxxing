A working genealogy of face-recognition camouflage.

The Ghostmaxxing references archive maps artistic, academic, and activist work around face obfuscation, adversarial makeup, physical-world attacks, and anti-surveillance aesthetics. Each entry documents what was demonstrated, what its limits are, and whether Ghostmaxxing can directly or partially retest the idea.
