Functional guide · Ghostyle Transfer

Move the visible intervention, not the claim.

Extract paint from a before and after pair, align it to a target, and export a visual preview.

Transfer is an image-compositing tool. Its output can help plan or communicate a pattern. It does not test whether the target image evades detection or matching.

Inputs

Three images, two roles.

SlotUseSelection advice
BeforeThe same workshop face before the visible intervention. It is optional, but improves isolation.Match pose, crop, light, and expression with After as closely as possible.
AfterThe face carrying the paint or intervention that will be extracted.Use a clear, consented image with the full intervention visible.
TargetThe face that receives the extracted visual pattern.Use another image only when you have permission to process and publish it.

Before and After should represent a controlled pair. If background, light, hair, pose, or camera processing changes, those differences can enter the matte as false paint.

Minimum procedure

Mark, tune, transfer.

01

Choose the images

Load After and Target. Add Before when you have a corresponding bare-face image.

02

Draw one face box per image

Drag from one corner to the opposite corner around the face. Keep hair and background outside where possible.

03

Choose the extraction

Paint sensitivity controls how much visible difference is treated as paint. Edge feather softens the matte boundary. Opacity and Blend control the preview.

04

Keep mesh alignment enabled first

Follow features (mesh) attempts to align the pattern through detected facial landmarks. If that cannot be completed, the tool can use the boxes you drew.

05

Select Transfer Ghostyle

Inspect the transferred result, extracted paint panel, and reported alignment mode.

06

Download only after inspection

Look for background leakage, doubled features, and paint displaced from eyes, nose, or mouth. Adjust the boxes or controls, then rerun.

Quality checks

Inspect what entered the matte.

  • Paint should remain attached to the intended facial regions.
  • Skin, hair, clothing, and background changes should not dominate the extracted layer.
  • Eye, mouth, and nose placement should remain plausible on the target.
  • The reported mode should match what the interface actually used.
  • The exported image should retain enough resolution for its intended next step.
Data and limits

Local processing still needs consent.

The tool runs in the browser and does not need to upload the three images to produce a result. A local operation can still expose or reproduce a person's face. Use consented inputs, remove unnecessary metadata, and do not publish a target result merely because the computation stayed on your device.

The transfer output is a design preview. To evaluate recognition behaviour, use the resulting pattern in a separate, controlled Lab or Loader experiment and state those conditions.
