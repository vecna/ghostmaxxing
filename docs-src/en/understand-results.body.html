Read the Lab history

The Lab compares each usable camera observation with the numbered faces saved in this browser. The top bar shows the nearest reference number and its Euclidean descriptor distance. Lower distances mean closer matches under the local model.

ColorMeaning
RedDistance at or below 0.58.
YellowDistance above 0.58 and at or below 0.63.
GreenDistance above 0.63.
Gray stripesThe current observation has no usable face descriptor.

The view shows eight seconds of history. Compare the reported reference number with the expected person, especially if you saved several faces. Missing observations carry no distance.

Read each tool on its own terms

  • Lab measures live camera frames and compares them with saved face references. Its 2D landmarks and 3D mesh show face geometry.
  • Realtime follows descriptor drift and distances across the marked steps of one physical-makeup session.
  • Ghostyle Player previews the digital design. Use the Local Fuzzer report to interpret pose-level tests of generated designs.
  • Video Loader exposes both face-api distance and experimental image-embedding similarity in its diagnostic details. These measures use different scales.
Record a useful result

Record the test conditions

  • Tool and application revision.
  • Input file or camera, resolution, frame rate, and controlled timestamp where available.
  • Model files, browser, and inference environment.
  • Baseline identity within the test, without publishing the raw biometric descriptor.
  • Makeup step or Ghostyle and parameters, pose, camera distance, light, and threshold.
  • Whether detection returned a face and which input produced the displayed value.
  • A series or range of observations, not only one favourable frame.
