Every way a machine has learned to read a face has, sooner or later, been read back. The tree grows bottom-up: oldest at the roots, each era carrying the camera that enforced it and the intervention that defeated it.

ERA OF DEPTH · 2017 — 2020 DEPTH SENSOR ARRAY 2017 — 2020 Depth & near-infrared Dot projectors and infrared sensors read the face as 3D geometry — in the dark, and with a liveness check a photograph cannot pass. DEFEATED IN 2021 Adv-Makeup · NIR-LED illumination attacks Limits: reported against the authors' own models and lighting. ERA OF LEARNING · 2014 — 2016 IOT HOME CAMERA 2014 — 2016 CNN face embeddings DeepFace and FaceNet stop measuring features and start learning them: every face becomes a vector in a space nobody designed by hand. DEFEATED IN 2016 Adversarial eyeglass frames — Sharif et al. Limits: optimised against named models; retraining closes most of the gap. ERA OF MEASUREMENT · 2010 — 2013 DOME CAMERA 2010 — 2013 Landmark geometry Eigenfaces, Fisherfaces, 68-point meshes. The face is a set of distances between named points — and the dome never shows you which way it looks. DEFEATED IN 2014 URME prosthetic — Leo Selvaggio Limits: hides the points rather than fooling them. Highly visible to humans. ERA OF CONTRAST · 2001 — 2009 BULLET CCTV 2001 — 2009 Haar cascades Viola-Jones slides rectangles of light and dark across the frame. Cheap, fast, everywhere — and it only ever looked for contrast. DEFEATED IN 2010 CV Dazzle — Adam Harvey Limits: tuned to the detectors of its day; ineffective against the CNNs that replaced them.
