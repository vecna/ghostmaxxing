1. Scope and source files

This guide covers Ghostmaxxing software and its public website. Ghòstati! is the initiative of NINA's Universal Union in both languages. The Union organises workshops and manages confidential whistleblowing. Its editorial site has its own visual identity. A hosted Ghostmaxxing tool can retain the software interface.

Use brand voice and outreach for writing rules, attribution and integration instructions. This page replaces the visual-direction tutorial. It is a working reference, not an accessibility certification.

2. Voice: write an action or an observation

Write so a reader can understand what the tool does and what a result means. Keep criticism of biometric surveillance specific. Do not turn technical uncertainty into a slogan.

  • No em dashes in new prose. Use full stops, commas or colons.
  • No abstract hyperbole: remove “revolutionary”, “transformative” and “game-changing”.
  • No convoluted introductions, invented profundity or “not just X, but Y” hooks.
  • Name the model, action, receiving organisation and relevant limitation.
  • Use sentence-case headings and concrete labels. Do not make every sentence a headline.
Describe the action
Avoid Unlock the transformative power of adversarial beauty.
Use Apply a Ghostyle and compare it with your saved baseline.
Describe the measurement
Avoid Your face is now invisible.
Use The detector found no face in this frame. This does not establish protection against other systems.
Name the recipient
Avoid Send evidence to our anonymous backend.
Use Read NINA's guidance for confidential reporting through Ghòstati!.
Give a usable design instruction
Avoid The greens are ordered, and the order is the contract.
Use Draw camera outlines with --gm-green. Fill the lens barrel with --gm-green-deep.

These examples remain full-width rows on narrow screens. Long copy must wrap; do not reduce its font size to preserve columns.

Pink needs a visible edge

Open lab

Use ink text and a 2px ink border for the pink CTA. Use cream or dark green behind small pink marks. Do not use pink body text, a pink page background or an unbounded pink signal on orange.

Yellow denotes a live decorative camera iris; do not assign it as the identity colour of the Fediverse page. Runtime tools have separate measurement roles, described below. Colour always needs a text label when it communicates a result.

The --gm-smoke* ramp supports existing painted motifs only. Do not use it as an alternative general palette.

Use --t-display, --t-h1, --t-h2 and related tokens for fluid display sizes. --t-body is 1rem and stays readable rather than shrinking with the viewport. Use the token font stacks, including fallbacks; fonts are self-hosted under styles/vendor/.

5. Dark technical tools

lab.html loads styles/lab.css; realtime.html also loads styles/realtime.css. Their dark surfaces keep controls visible over camera imagery. This is an intentional tool design, not a second theme for editorial pages.

The frame loads the real stylesheets in a separate document. Loading lab.css into this guide's parent document would change global tokens, background and overflow. The specimen runs no tool code and requests no camera access.

6. Mark, cameras and image use

Use existing images/logo/mark-*.svg assets. Choose the painted light/dark variant for the background and the small cut at about 28px or below. Keep clear space of approximately one lens radius. Do not rotate or recolour individual lenses.

Preserve the mark's two upper live lenses, pink gust and lower spent lens. If animated, only the gust moves; the static mark must remain understandable.

The camera fleet

The bullet camera appears in section 3. All six sources live in images/icons/cam-*.svg. Shared drawing and states come from styles/cameras.css; page CSS positions them. Keep the 100 × 100 viewBox. Derive iris and glint geometry from the lens radius: 0.44 for the iris, 0.17 for the glint radius and 0.30 for its offset.

Live uses a yellow iris; reading uses pink; spent uses a dark iris and pink residue. These are drawing states. When used in an argument, explain the evidence they represent. A spent decorative lens is not proof that a model or technology family has been defeated.

  • Inline class-driven camera SVGs so CSS reaches their internal parts. Keep repeated IDs unique.
  • Use <img> for painted logo assets. Parent-page currentColor does not enter an external SVG document.
  • Give informative images useful alternative text; hide purely decorative repetition from assistive technology.
  • Do not add a logo or sigil inside a camera lens, or create separate “alive” icon files.
  • Use real experiment images with consent and context. Avoid targeting reticles, scanline overload and generic cybersecurity stock art.

These inline camera specimens are copies of the source assets. Update them when those assets change. The former curved-stamp experiment is not a production dependency; do not invent a shared lab-js/stamp.js to support it.

7. Pages, navigation and motion

Editorial pages use flat orange ground, cream reading surfaces, square panels and visible ink borders. Do not introduce decorative gradients, blurred shadows or a dashboard card stack. The pink CTA pill, camera geometry and dark tool controls are deliberate exceptions.

  • Homepage: one clear introduction and route into the lab. A camera composition must support the explanation, not obscure it.
  • About: explain software, public research and NINA's separate responsibilities. Distinguish implemented functions from plans.
  • Archive: make dates, authors, source, demonstrated result, conditions and limits easy to scan.
  • Fediverse: explain ActivityPub infrastructure, publication and moderation. Use calm reading surfaces; link a real identity profile with rel="me" only where appropriate, not the local explainer.

Current genealogy

scripts-dev/build-genealogy.py generates the page and CSS from references/REFERENCES.json and projects/PROJECTS.json. It places dated entries against technology families and provides wide and narrow SVG layouts. A marker records a documented work, not a universal defeat. Edit the data or generator, run npm run update:genealogy, and check both layouts and source links.

The retired centred tree, alternating dossiers and smoke spine are not the current layout contract. Existing painted motifs may remain useful elsewhere; do not restore that layout while editing the current generator.

Navigation maintenance

Keep the agreed content grouping: Tools, Explore, Ghòstati! / NINA and the primary Open lab action. Use Makeup Explorer and Fediverse integration as short labels. Label NINA destinations so the change of organisation is clear. Tools use compact navigation so the camera remains usable.

Navbar and footer markup may be duplicated in authored HTML and generator templates. Update all consumers together, including projects, references and genealogy templates/generators. Preserve current-page markers and relative paths. Do not add another template generator solely for this guide.

Interaction checks

  • Keep links and essential information usable without animation.
  • Support keyboard opening, Escape closing and visible focus in menus.
  • Under reduced motion, show the informative state immediately; do not remove the state with the animation.
  • Test narrow layouts, long titles and zoom. Do not shrink body text to fit a fixed composition.
  • --gm-focus-ring is defined in tokens.css. Check its contrast on each surface; dark tools may require a light focus treatment.

8. Previews, metadata and sitemap

A preview must identify the software and the page's task. It must not promise that a user becomes invisible or protected. Keep workshop ownership and reporting recipients correct in metadata as well as visible text.

Example page metadata copy

Makeup Explorer | Ghostmaxxing

Compare a live camera view with a saved baseline and inspect how visual changes affect the model's output. Results depend on the test conditions.

  1. Edit scripts-dev/social-cards.html and run node scripts-dev/build-social-cards.cjs --only generic for the generic card.
  2. Review the exported files and cards-manifest.json under images/social/. Supported formats include 1200 × 630 landscape, 1200 × 1200 square and 1500 × 500 wide layouts.
  3. Keep one short title, one factual sentence and a relevant image. Keep essential text away from crop edges. Test thumbnail legibility and actual platform cropping.
  4. Update title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card and relevant JSON-LD together. Use absolute public URLs and actual image dimensions/alt text.
  5. Use real authors and publishers in JSON-LD. Do not claim NINA publishes the software, or invent ratings, results and release dates.

Use the correct document language and link actual translations with hreflang. Generated metadata needs review too: changing this guide does not update scripts-dev/enrich-jsdoc-seo.cjs or existing social pictures.

Sitemap ownership

Maintain web-files/sitemap.xml; the client installer copies it into served output. npm run htmls does not generate a complete sitemap. Include canonical public pages intended for indexing, remove deleted routes and omit internal guides, previews and receipt fragments. Use real content modification dates. Check robots.txt against the deployed sitemap location.

At the reviewed commit, realtime and its functional guide need a separate sitemap audit. This internal styleguide remains noindex and should not be listed.

9. External hosting: name each operator

NINA or another organisation may serve Ghostmaxxing's interface locally while using the upstream public backend. The frontend hostname is not the identity of every service used by that page.

OperationOwner / destination
Serving the interface, assets and modelsThe host serving the copied package.
Local comparisonThe participant’s browser; keep the distinction from network resource loading clear.
Consented upload, public gallery and mediaGhostmaxxing public backend, routed through lab-js/backend-url.js.
Ghòstati! workshops and confidential reportsNINA's Universal Union; link Sindacato's maintained guidance.
Receipt links and local receipt storageThe frontend origin/path used by the participant; do not promise portability between hosts.

The backend helper selects http://localhost:4040 on localhost/loopback, the current origin on ghostmaxxing.vecna.eu, and https://ghostmaxxing.vecna.eu on other hosts. Keep asset-path rewriting separate from API routing.

Before release, check HTTPS/camera permissions, nested asset paths, CORS, CSP and upload/deletion behaviour from the actual host. Make consent and the receiving service visible. Do not infer working remote uploads from working local inference.

Use the host's page identity consistently in canonical, Open Graph, structured data and sitemap when that page is intended to be indexed independently. For an unchanged mirror, explicitly decide whether upstream is canonical. Backend origin does not choose canonical URL.

On Sindacato, a lab link intended to preserve the editorial page opens with target="_blank" rel="noopener". Other external links need clear labels, not an automatic new-tab policy. See brand voice for attribution examples and the integration checklist.

10. Maintenance and review

  • Keep exact values in CSS, writing rules in brand voice and visual examples here. Do not restore a parallel visual-direction policy.
  • When changing colour or typography, review editorial pages, both dark tools, the loader and social exports. Their surfaces differ.
  • When changing routes or ownership, review visible copy, navigation templates, metadata, JSON-LD, sitemap and integration documentation together.
  • When changing an experimental claim, review its model, conditions and evidence; metadata must not overstate it.
  • Check this page at narrow width and 200% zoom, with keyboard navigation and reduced motion. Verify fonts, assets, frame content and focus contrast.

Remove tutorials/visual-direction.md and its catalogue entry from tutorials/tutorials.json, update authored references, then run npm run docs:rebuild. Clean stale deployed tutorial output as well.
